### Reporting content or copyright concerns

News Observer does not write or host articles. It shows headlines, short
descriptions and links received from third-party news services. If you believe
an item shown in the app is unlawful, inaccurate, harmful or infringes your
rights (including copyright or trademark), email the developer with:

1. the link to the original article, and a screenshot or the headline as shown in the app;
2. a description of the problem;
3. for rights claims — who you are, which right is affected, and confirmation
   that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

Every report is reviewed, usually within 14 days. Where a report is justified,
the developer will stop showing the item or the source in the app. Please note
that the developer cannot remove the article from the publisher's website or
from the third-party news services.

## Privacy Policy {#privacy-policy}

This policy explains what data the News Observer Android app ("the app")
handles. The app is published by Denis Ians ("the developer", "I").

### Summary

- The developer **does not collect, receive or store** any of your personal data.
  There is no developer server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no
  crash-reporting SDK.
- To show news, the app sends requests **directly from your device** to
  third-party news services (listed below).
- Your saved articles, search history and settings are stored **only on your
  device**.

### Data sent to third-party news services

To load and search news, the app connects over encrypted HTTPS to:

| Service | What is sent | Their privacy policy / terms |
|---|---|---|
| NewsData.io | Search query you type; selected language, region and category; page token | [Privacy Policy](https://newsdata.io/privacy-policy), [Terms](https://newsdata.io/terms-and-conditions) |
| The GDELT Project | Search query you type, including the selected language and region filters | [Terms of Use](https://www.gdeltproject.org/about.html#termsofuse) |

As with any internet request, these services also receive technical
information such as your IP address, the request time and the app's HTTP client
identifier. The developer has no access to this information and does not
control how these services process it — please read their policies.

These requests happen when you open or refresh the feed, search, and — only if
you turn on daily article notifications — once a day in the background.

### Data stored on your device

| Data | Purpose | How to delete |
|---|---|---|
| Downloaded article metadata (headline, short description, publisher, authors, date, link) | Show the feed offline and quickly | *Settings → Clear cache*; replaced when the feed refreshes; deleted automatically 30 days after the feed was last loaded |
| Saved articles | Your favourites list (kept until you remove them) | Remove from *Saved*, or uninstall the app |
| Recent searches (last 10) | Search suggestions | Remove in the search screen, or uninstall the app |
| Settings (sources, language, region, notifications) | Remember your choices | Uninstall the app or clear app data in Android settings |

This data never leaves your device through the app. It is excluded from
Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied
to Google or to a new phone.

Article metadata may include names of people mentioned in the news. The app
only shows what publishers have already made public and does not store full
article text or images.

### Opening and sharing articles

- **Opening an article** opens the publisher's website in your browser (via
  Chrome Custom Tabs or your default browser). From that point the publisher's
  website and your browser's privacy policies apply, including their cookies
  and ads.
- **Sharing** sends the article headline and link to the app you choose.
- **Emailing the developer** opens your email app. If you write, I receive your
  email address and message and use them only to reply and handle your request.
  Messages are kept no longer than needed for that purpose.

### Permissions

- **Internet and network state** — to load news.
- **Notifications** (optional, Android 13+) — to show one article per day if you
  turn this on in settings. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in
  Android settings.

### Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect data about app
installs and usage as described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer
does not receive personal data from Google beyond the aggregated, anonymous
statistics Google Play Console provides to all developers.

### Children

The app is a general news app and is not directed at children under 13. News
may include topics such as war, violence and crime.

### Your rights

Because the developer does not hold your personal data, there is nothing to
access, correct or delete on the developer's side — all app data is on your
device and you control it as described above. For data processed by NewsData.io,
the GDELT Project or Google, contact those services directly. If you have
emailed the developer, you can ask for your correspondence to be deleted.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the Law of
the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection" or the EU
General Data Protection Regulation. Contact
[iansubd@gmail.com](mailto:iansubd@gmail.com) with any request.

### Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before
the new version is released and the effective date above will change.
